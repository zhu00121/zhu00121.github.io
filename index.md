## About Me

I am currently a researhc scientist at Reality Defender. I have obtained my phd from <a href="https://musaelab.ca/" target="_blank"> Multisensory Signal Analysis and Enhancement Lab (MuSAE)</a>, <a href="https://inrs.ca/en/" target="_blank"> National Institute of Scientific Research (INRS)</a> (Montreal, Canada) in Feb 2025.

My recent research has been centered around self-supervised learning, speech and audio foundation model pretraining, and the downstream applications of audio language models. Previously I have also worked closely with different sensor signals, such as physiological signals (e.g., EMG), humidity, temperature, etc.
